SongChords can display **chord diagrams** to quickly show fingering shapes for guitar chords.  
This is especially helpful when learning new songs or quickly looking up unfamiliar chords.

---

## Displaying chord diagrams

* Chord diagrams can be shown by tapping or clicking on them.
* If multiple variants exist, you can view them by swiping left or right.

---

## Supported chords

SongChords supports a wide range of chord types, including:

* major and minor chords  
* seventh, major seventh, and minor seventh chords  
* sus, add, and slash chords  
* extended chords like 9, 11, or 13  

Unknown or very specific chords are simplified whenever possible or shown without a diagram.

---

## Appearance and layout

The diagrams use a compact, minimal presentation:

* vertical lines correspond to strings  
* horizontal lines correspond to frets  
* dots mark finger positions (1=index, 2=middle, 3=ring, 4=little)  
* an x marks muted strings  
* an o marks open strings  

---

## Tip

> If a chord feels unfamiliar or hard to finger, it can help to practice it slowly using the diagram first, and only then use it in the song context

> Chord diagrams are a visual aid. Musical interpretation and rhythm remain essential
