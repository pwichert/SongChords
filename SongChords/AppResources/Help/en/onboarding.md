# Quick Start

This short introduction shows how to **get to a playable song as quickly as possible** – without any lengthy setup.

---

## Select a Playlist

In order for a song to be visible, a **playlist must be selected**:

*1 Select a playlist on the left or create one using {icon:plus.circle}.  
*2 The songs contained in the playlist are displayed.  
*3 Tap on a song to display it.

Without a selected playlist, the song list remains empty.

---

## The Fastest Way: Paste from the Clipboard

First, select a playlist.  
If you already have a song text with chords somewhere (e.g. from a website, text file, or email):

*1 Copy the song text to the **clipboard**.  
*2 Open SongChords.  
*3 Select a playlist.  
*4 In the menu ({icon:ellipsis.circle}), choose `Import text from clipboard`.  
   You may be asked for permission.  
*5 **Done.** You now have a new song in the list.

> Tip: Simple text formats with chords above the lines or chords in brackets are usually recognized correctly.

---

## Importing a ChordPro File

If you already have a **ChordPro file** (`.cho`, `.chordpro`, `.pro`):

*1 Select a playlist.  
*2 In the menu ({icon:ellipsis.circle}), choose `Import ChordPro file`.  
*2 Select the ChordPro file.

The song is immediately ready to play and will be shown in the live view.

---

## Adjust the Display

After importing, you can adjust the song right away:

* Show or hide chords  
* Change the font size  
* Display chord diagrams  

This prepares the song optimally – whether for practice, rehearsal, or performance.

---

## Ready to Play

Once a song is open, you can:

* Activate autoscroll  
* Use the audio player  
* Or display the song as a PDF  

Now your hands stay free for your instrument 🎸🎹

---

## Tip

If you frequently import new songs:

* Create a playlist like **New imports**  
* Sort the songs into your target playlists later by dragging or copying the song into the new list

This keeps you fast and organized.

---

Have fun with SongChords  
and enjoy playing! 🎶
